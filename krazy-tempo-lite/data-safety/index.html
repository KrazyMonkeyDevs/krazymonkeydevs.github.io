Krazy Tempo Lite

Data Safety Statement

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Android package: com.krazymonkeydevs.krazytempo.lite

Your own music. Your chosen tempo. Your data stays under your control.

In brief

  • No account or sign-in required.
  • No in-app advertising or advertising profiles.
  • No automatic upload of your music library, playback history or app settings to Krazy Monkey Devs.
  • Optional support ZIP: it is generated locally; you choose whether to send it separately.

What Krazy Tempo Lite handles

Support email address, message and attachmentRespond to your requestReceived by Krazy Monkey Devs only if you send an email

What happens if you contact support?

Saving a ZIP is not the same as sending one. Krazy Tempo Lite prepares the file locally. If you later send it by email, we receive the information in that message and attachment and use it to investigate your issue. The email services involved process correspondence under their own policies.

How to remove information

Clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove most local app records. Delete any separately saved ZIP files yourself. For information you sent to us by email, request deletion at KrazyMonkeyDevs@yahoo.com.
