Krazy Tempo Lite

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Android package: com.krazymonkeydevs.krazytempo.lite

Your music is yours. Krazy Tempo Lite uses your local library to support the tempo you choose.

1. Developer and contact

This policy describes the Android app Krazy Tempo Lite, developed by Krazy Monkey Devs. For privacy enquiries or questions about support material you have sent us, contact KrazyMonkeyDevs@yahoo.com.

2. Music and device data we access

To locate and play your music, Krazy Tempo Lite accesses audio files and related metadata available through the Android permissions and folders you authorize. These may include track titles, artists, albums, filenames or paths, audio formats, durations and existing BPM (beats per minute) tags.

It also uses app preferences, playlist information, playback state and recent session records to provide playback controls, BPM-based selection and troubleshooting.

3. Local processing

Music-library indexing, BPM-based selection, playlist generation and playback are designed to operate on your device. App preferences, library indexes and recent session records are stored locally.

You do not need to create a Krazy Monkey Devs account, subscribe to a streaming service or connect to the internet to play music in the app.

4. Automatic collection, advertising and tracking

Krazy Tempo Lite does not intentionally transmit your audio files, music metadata, playback history or app preferences to Krazy Monkey Devs. It does not display advertising or build advertising profiles.

The Android operating system and your device's storage and media services may process information to provide standard device functions. Those services are governed by their applicable policies.

5. Optional support reports and email

When you request a support report, Krazy Tempo Lite generates a ZIP file on your device. The report contains your issue description, app and Android versions, device details, diagnostic logs and recent session information.

Diagnostic records may contain internal identifiers, but do not include google account information, music titles, filenames or folder paths. Such information would only be received if you choose to provide it in your report or attach additional files.

Creating or saving a support ZIP does not send it to us. If you independently choose to attach the ZIP to an email, Krazy Monkey Devs receives your message, your sending email address and whatever you attach. We use that information to understand and respond to your support request. Please review the ZIP before sending it if you have privacy concerns.

Emails travel through your chosen email service and our email provider, which apply their own privacy and security practices. Krazy Tempo Lite does not provide an automatic diagnostic upload service.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Your original audio files remain in their normal storage location. Clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling the app generally removes its local settings, indexes and recent session records, subject to Android and device backup behaviour. Support ZIPs saved outside the app's private storage may remain after uninstallation; you can delete those files yourself.

Support emails and attachments you voluntarily send are retained only as reasonably necessary to handle the request and any related follow-up, and are deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable legal or security obligations. You can ask us to review or delete the support information you submitted by contacting KrazyMonkeyDevs@yahoo.com. We may need enough details to locate the relevant correspondence.

7. Security

The app's normal music and playlist processing takes place locally rather than through a Krazy Monkey Devs music server. Support messages are handled through email services. No electronic storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. External websites

If you use the in-app Privacy Policy link, your browser opens this public website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information as described in its Privacy Statement. Your browser and internet provider may also process connection information.

9. Children

Krazy Tempo Lite is a general-purpose local music application. It is not specifically designed for children and does not offer social networking or account registration.

10. Changes

We may revise this policy if the app or our support practices change. We will publish the current version here and update the effective date.
